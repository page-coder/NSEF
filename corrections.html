Corrections policy

NSEF aims to distinguish sourced fact, interpretation, and proposal. If you find an error, identify the page, the specific claim, and a source that supports your correction where possible. Submissions are reviewed against primary or otherwise credible sources. Material confirmed errors should be corrected with a dated note where practical. Disagreement over policy interpretation may be reflected as a limitation or counterargument rather than treated as a factual correction.
