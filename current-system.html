The current Indian system

Public support is not a single system. Reservation, scholarships, fee support, welfare, and anti-discrimination protections do different things.

Keep the tools distinct

ToolPlain-language purpose
ReservationConstitutionally structured provisions affecting seats or public employment opportunities in specified contexts.
Scholarships and fee supportFinancial support for education; rules and recipients vary by scheme.
Income-based welfareAssistance linked to household conditions or other criteria.
Anti-discrimination protectionsLegal and institutional measures addressing exclusion and unequal treatment.

Why a single eligibility chart would mislead

Central and state arrangements differ, and scheme rules can change. NSEF does not publish one nationwide eligibility table. Before relying on a programme, consult the responsible official department and the applicable current notification.

Constitutional context

Provisions concerning SCs, STs, OBCs and Economically Weaker Sections have different legal foundations and purposes. Read the legal guide for primary links and limitations.

Source starting point: India Code, which hosts statutory and constitutional texts. Scheme rules should be checked with the relevant official authority.
