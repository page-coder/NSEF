Frequently asked questions

What is NSEF?

An independent policy research proposal about how different barriers to opportunity might be examined.

Is NSEF a government initiative?

No. It is not a government department, approved reform, or political party platform.

Does NSEF examine the concerns of Brahmins and other General Category people?

Yes. It examines economic hardship and access barriers without assuming any group is economically uniform.

Does it dismiss caste discrimination?

No. It treats discrimination, historical exclusion, and representation as analytically distinct from household poverty.

Does NSEF propose abolishing existing reservations?

No. It does not make that proposal or claim authority to do so.

Is the framework legally approved?

No. It is under examination. Read Constitution & law.

How can I correct an error?

Use the feedback form and select “Research correction.”
