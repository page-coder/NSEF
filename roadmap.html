A cautious, evidence-led roadmap

This is a proposed sequence, not an approved government programme.

  1. Research and evidence collection: define questions and identify data limits.
  2. Legal and constitutional review: test specific ideas, not broad assumptions.
  3. Stakeholder consultation: include affected communities, practitioners, and critics.
  4. Pilot design: establish clear safeguards and measurable outcomes.
  5. Independent evaluation: assess effects, access, errors, and unintended consequences.
  6. Revision and public reporting: publish methods, findings, and limits.
  7. Wider consideration only if justified: proceed only where evidence, legality, feasibility, and safeguards support it.
